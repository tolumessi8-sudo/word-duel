**Full description:**
Word Duel is a fast, head-to-head word ladder race.

You and your rival start from the same word and race to reach the same target, changing ONE letter at a time. FIRE → FIRS → FIRM → FORM → FOAM... first to the target wins!

• Real-time duels - 2-minute matches, matched with players near your trophy level
• 7 divisions from Rookie to Legend - climb the ranks and earn promotion rewards
• Photo finishes - finish within a second of your rival and it's a draw
• Weekend matches - ties are decided by fewest moves
• Daily puzzle, plus Challenge a Friend with a room code
• Events and rewards, coins for hints and cosmetics
• Customise your avatar and frame, send quick emotes (GG, WOW, OOPS...)
• Quick tutorial so you can start winning in a minute

Free to play, with optional rewarded ads for bonus coins.
