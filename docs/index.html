Word Duel - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Word Duel ("the game") is a word-ladder duel game published by MS Games.

What we collect

  • Player name you choose. It is shown to other players on leaderboards and in matches. Please do not use your real name.
  • Anonymous player ID and secret key generated on your device so we can keep your progress. No account, email or phone number is needed.
  • Gameplay data: matches, words played, trophies, coins, division, purchases of in-game cosmetics, emotes sent.

Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and similar data to serve and measure ads, and to personalise them where allowed. See Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

How data is stored

Game data is stored on Supabase servers (EU region) and sent over encrypted connections. We do not sell your data.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Ask us to delete your player record by opening an issue at github.com/tolumessi8-sudo/word-duel/issues with your player name. Uninstalling the game removes the local ID.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
